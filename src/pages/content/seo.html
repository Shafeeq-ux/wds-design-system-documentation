Pattern purpose

Search engines and social platforms read a page's title, description, headings and structured data to decide how to show it. Good metadata gets the right people to the right page.

Voice and tone

Titles and descriptions are ads in search results. Lead with what the searcher wants, in the same clear voice as the page.

Writing rules

ElementRuleLimit
Title tagUnique per page. Page topic first, then "| Tekion".60 characters
Meta descriptionOne or two sentences: what the page offers and why to click. Include the main search phrase naturally.155 characters
H1One per page, close in meaning to the title tag.60 characters
URL slugLowercase words joined by hyphens, no dates or IDs: /automotive-retail-cloud/service3–5 words
Open Graph titleCan be warmer than the title tag. No "| Tekion".60 characters
Open Graph image1200 × 630px, key text inside the central 1000 × 500px, under 1MB.—
Alt textSee Alt text.125 characters
  • Use headings in order (h1 → h2 → h3), with real words in them. Search engines use them to understand the page.
  • Link between related pages with descriptive link text.
  • Add FAQ structured data (FAQPage) only for questions that are visible on the page.
  • Don't hide copy in closed accordions or tabs if the page needs to rank for it. Put a summary in visible text too.

Approved examples

Approved

  • Title: "AI-Native Dealer Management System | Tekion" (43)
  • Description: "Run sales, service, parts and accounting on one AI-native platform. See how Tekion ARC helps dealerships save time and sell more." (129)

Not approved

  • Title: "Home" or "Tekion | Tekion | Tekion"
  • Description: the same text on every page

Accessibility and localisation notes

  • The title tag is the first thing a screen reader announces on a new page, so it must describe the page (WCAG 2.4.2).
  • Translate titles, descriptions and Open Graph text for each language version, and link versions with hreflang.
