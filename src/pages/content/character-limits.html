Pattern purpose

Every text slot in a WDS section has a fixed width and a set number of lines before the layout breaks. Character limits turn that into a number writers can work to, so copy fits at every breakpoint without redesigns or last-minute cuts.

How limits are calculated

  1. Take the slot's text style and its maximum width on desktop, measured in Figma.
  2. Estimate characters per line: width ÷ (font size × 0.45 × font width). Tekion Sans averages about 0.45em per character. Headlines are set at 110% width, body at 105% and statistics at 125%, which makes characters wider.
  3. Multiply by the allowed lines, keep 95% to leave room for word wrap, and round down to the nearest 5.
  4. Check the same text at the mobile size and the 342px mobile width, to see how many lines it will take there.

The 0.45em figure was calibrated against the longest real copy in Figma. For example, "Connected Intelligence. Seamless Consumer Experiences." (54 characters) fits two lines of Headline 2 at 580px.

Writing rules

  • Write to the limit at mobile width, not just desktop. A two-line desktop H1 can take four lines on a phone.
  • Limits are maximums, not targets. The best headlines are usually well under.
  • Count spaces and punctuation. The checkers do.
  • Keep parallel items similar in length. Cards in a row look broken when one has twice as much copy.
  • Don't fix overflow with a smaller style. Shorten the copy, or pick a section variant built for more text.

Approved examples

Fits

  • Hero H1, 51 characters: "The AI-Native Platform for Modern Automotive Retail"
  • Card title, 25 characters: "Operate as One Enterprise"

Too long

  • Hero H1, 87 characters: "The Only Truly AI-Native Platform Built From the Ground Up for Modern Automotive Retail"
  • CTA, 37 characters: "Schedule Your Personalized Demo Today"

Accessibility and localisation notes

  • Plan for 30% expansion when copy will be translated. Aim for 70% of the limit in English.
  • People who zoom to 200% or override text spacing see fewer characters per line (WCAG 1.4.4, 1.4.12). Layouts wrap, and limits that leave room for wrapping keep the page usable.
  • Never truncate copy with an ellipsis to make it fit. Rewrite it.
