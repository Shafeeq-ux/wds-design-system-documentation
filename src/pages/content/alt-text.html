Pattern purpose

Alt text describes an image for people who can't see it: screen reader users, people on slow connections, and search engines. Every image needs a decision: describe it, or mark it as decorative (WCAG 1.1.1).

Voice and tone

Plain and factual. Describe what matters in this context, the way you'd describe it to a colleague on the phone.

Writing rules

Image typeWhat to write
Product screenshotWhat the screen shows and why it matters: "T1 answering a sales manager's question about today's pipeline"
Photo of peopleWho, doing what, where, if it's relevant: "Dealers comparing notes at a Tekion One roundtable"
Chart or diagramThe takeaway, with the data in nearby text or a table: "Bar chart showing total AI revenue reaching $235M in 2025"
Customer or partner logoThe company name only: "Penske Automotive"
Compliance badgeThe certification: "ISO/IEC 27001 certified"
Linked image or logoWhere the link goes: "Tekion home"
Decorative art, blobs, gradientsEmpty alt (alt="") or aria-hidden="true"
Icon next to a text labelDecorative: aria-hidden="true"
  • Don't start with "Image of" or "Picture of". Screen readers already say it's an image.
  • Don't repeat the caption or nearby heading word for word.
  • Put text that's inside an image into the alt text, but avoid images of text in the first place.
  • End with a full stop so screen readers pause.

Character or word limits

125 characters at most. Some screen readers cut longer alt text. If an image needs more (a complex diagram, for example), describe it in the page copy or a caption and keep the alt text short.

Approved examples

Approved

  • alt="Service advisor reviewing a repair order recommendation in Tekion ARC."
  • alt="SOC 2 compliant"
  • alt="" on the gradient blobs in a banner

Not approved

  • alt="image123.jpg"
  • alt="Image of a screen"
  • No alt attribute at all

Accessibility and localisation notes

  • Alt text is translated with the page, so keep it in the CMS with the rest of the copy, not baked into templates.
  • Video needs captions and, for important visual detail, audio description or a text alternative (WCAG 1.2).
  • Background images set in CSS have no alt text. If one carries meaning, it shouldn't be a background.
