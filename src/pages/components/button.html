Overview

Buttons trigger an action or move people to the next step. WDS has seven types in one Figma component set, each in four states: Default, Hover, Pressed and Disabled (28 variants).

TypeUse forSize
PrimaryThe single most important action in a section, such as "Request demo".56px high, pill
SecondaryAn alternative next to a primary button, such as "See the platform".56px high, pill, 2px border
TertiaryA lighter action inside cards, banners and lists.Label + 24px circle
LinkNavigation in running text or between pages ("Previous", "Read more").16px high
Icon large / medium / smallA compact action with a familiar icon: menu, play, carousel arrows.56 / 40 / 32px circles

Variants and states

Hover over the live buttons to see the real interactions. The Hover and Pressed columns are forced so you can compare them side by side.

  • Primary uses an image fill (blurred blue and purple "aurora" blobs) for Default, Hover and Pressed. On hover a frosted blur follows the pointer. On press the blur switches off so the colours snap sharp, which makes the button feel pushed in.
  • Secondary fills with btn-secondary-bg-hover and darkens its border on hover, then deepens on press.
  • Tertiary and Link darken the label on hover. Link also underlines.
  • Disabled removes every effect and uses flat, muted colours.

Design guidance

Do

  • Use one primary button per section.
  • Pair primary with secondary, primary first (left, or on top on mobile).
  • Use tertiary or link inside cards so the section's main CTA stays clear.
  • Use icon-only buttons only for icons people already know.

Don't

  • Put two primary buttons side by side.
  • Recreate the primary fill with a CSS gradient. Use the image asset.
  • Use a button for navigation to another page in running text. Use a link.
  • Disable a button without telling people why the action isn't available.

Responsive behaviour

  • Button sizes don't change between breakpoints. Primary and secondary stay 56px high, which also suits touch.
  • In a CTA group (.wds-actions), buttons sit side by side on desktop and tablet with a 16px gap, and stack full-width on mobile (767px and below).
  • Labels never wrap. If a label doesn't fit at 342px, shorten it. See Microcopy and limits.

Light and dark theme

  • Primary keeps the same image and white label in both themes.
  • Secondary, tertiary and link invert: dark text and borders in light mode, white in dark mode.
  • Icon-only buttons are dark circles with light glyphs in light mode, and light circles with dark glyphs in dark mode.

Token mismatch. The btn-primary-* colour tokens describe a dark-gray button with white text (and white on white in dark mode). The Figma component doesn't use them for its fill: it uses the image. The CSS follows the component. The tokens should be updated or removed. See Known issues.

Microcopy and limits

TypeLimitExample
Primary, Secondary2–4 words, 25 characters maxRequest a Demo
Tertiary2–4 words, 30 characters maxTerms & Conditions

Icon-onlyAccessible name of 1–3 wordsaria-label="Play video"
  • Start with a verb: "Book a Demo", not "Demo Booking".
  • Use Title Case, as in Figma ("Request a Demo", "See AI in Action"), and no end punctuation. See CTA labels.
  • Say what happens: avoid "Click here", "Submit" and "Learn more" on their own.
  • Allow for translation: German and French labels run about 30% longer.

Limits are proposed and pending Marketing sign-off on the Character limits page.

<!-- Navigation: same look, link semantics -->
<a class="wds-btn wds-btn--secondary" href="/platform">See the platform</a>

<!-- Tertiary with its circle -->

<button class="wds-btn wds-btn--primary" type="button" aria-disabled="true">Submit</button>

Interaction timing

From the Figma dev handoff: Default → Hover 150ms ease-out, Hover → Default 100ms ease-in, Hover → Pressed instant, Pressed → Default 200ms ease-out. Changes to and from Disabled are instant.

Tokens

Accessibility

  • Semantics: use <button> for actions and <a href> for navigation, even when they look the same (WCAG 4.1.2).
  • Focus visible: a 2px #47a5ff ring 2px outside the button. This is proposed in the Figma handoff, because Figma has no Focus variant (WCAG 2.4.7).
  • Keyboard: Enter and Space activate buttons; Enter activates links (WCAG 2.1.1).
  • Target size: tertiary, link and small icon buttons get an invisible hit area of at least 24px (WCAG 2.5.8). Aim for 44px on touch.
  • Disabled: use aria-disabled="true" so the button stays in the tab order, and stop its click handler.
  • Label in name: an icon-only button's aria-label must describe the action. A visible label must be part of the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3).
  • Contrast: the white primary label is 5.5–6.8:1 on the deep blue at the left and centre of the image, but only 2.8:1 on the light blue at the right edge. Keep labels short so they sit on the darker part of the fill.

Acceptance checklist

  • Primary uses the image fill, not a gradient, and shows the hover blur.
  • Every type has Default, Hover, Pressed and Disabled, in light and dark.
  • Focus ring is visible on every type.
  • Icon-only buttons have an accessible name.
  • Disabled buttons use aria-disabled and do nothing on click.
  • CTA groups stack at 767px and below.
  • Labels are verb-first, Title Case and within the limit.
