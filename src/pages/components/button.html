Overview

Buttons trigger an action or move people to the next step. WDS has two Figma component sets. Button (125 variants) is a labelled button set by five properties: Hierarchy, Tone, Surface, Size and State. IconButton (54 variants) is an icon-only circle. See IconButton.

Variants and states

Hover over and tab through the live buttons to see the real interactions. The Hover, Pressed and Focus columns are forced, so you can compare them side by side. Switch the preview to dark to see each theme.

  • Primary · Brand uses an image fill (blurred blue and purple "aurora" blobs) for Default, Hover, Pressed, Focus and Loading. On hover a frosted blur follows the pointer. On press the blur switches off so the colours snap sharp, which makes the button feel pushed in.
  • Primary · Neutral darkens on hover (btn-primary-bg-hover) and again on press (btn-primary-bg-active).
  • Secondary fills with btn-secondary-bg-hover and darkens its border on hover, then deepens on press. On media, the glass fill gets stronger on hover and press.
  • Tertiary darkens the label and the circle on hover and press. On media, it dims to 72% opacity on press.
  • Link darkens the label and underlines it on hover.
  • Focus adds a 2px ring 2px outside the button, in btn-focus-ring, or btn-media-focus-ring (white) on media. On Tertiary and Link the ring sits 4px above and below and 8px to each side, with 8px corners.
  • Loading keeps the button's size and colours, hides the label and shows a spinner in the label colour: 18px on labelled buttons and 20px on icon buttons.
  • Disabled removes every effect and uses flat, muted colours.

Design guidance

Do

  • Use one Brand primary per view. Use Primary · Neutral when a second strong action is needed.
  • Pair primary with secondary, primary first (left, or on top on mobile).
  • Use the On media surface for buttons over photos and video, with a blur or scrim behind them.
  • Use Medium and Small in dense places such as cards, tables and banners. Keep Large for section CTAs.
  • Use tertiary or link inside cards so the section's main CTA stays clear.

Don't

  • Put two Brand primary buttons side by side.
  • Recreate the Brand fill with a CSS gradient. Use the image asset.
  • Use Default-surface buttons on imagery. Their colours aren't designed for it.
  • Disable a button without telling people why the action isn't available.
  • Leave a button in Loading with no other feedback. Announce the result when it finishes.

Responsive behaviour

  • Button sizes don't change between breakpoints. Choose the size for the context: Large for section CTAs, which also suits touch.
  • In a CTA group (.wds-actions), buttons sit side by side on desktop and tablet with a 16px gap, and stack full-width on mobile (767px and below).
  • Labels never wrap. If a label doesn't fit at 342px, shorten it. See Microcopy and limits.

Light and dark theme

  • Primary · Brand keeps the same image and white label in both themes.
  • Primary · Neutral inverts: gray-800 with white text in light mode, white with dark text in dark mode.
  • Secondary, tertiary and link invert: dark text and borders in light mode, white in dark mode.
  • On media buttons don't change with the theme. They're always white or glass, because they sit on imagery, not on the page.
  • IconButton · Neutral is a dark circle with a light glyph in light mode, and a light circle with a dark glyph in dark mode.

Microcopy and limits

TypeLimitExample
Primary, Secondary2–4 words, 25 characters maxRequest a Demo
Tertiary2–4 words, 30 characters maxTerms & Conditions
Link1–4 wordsRead the Case Study
Icon-onlyAccessible name of 1–3 wordsaria-label="Play video"
  • Start with a verb: "Book a Demo", not "Demo Booking".
  • Use Title Case, as in Figma ("Request a Demo", "See AI in Action"), and no end punctuation. See CTA labels.
  • Say what happens: avoid "Click here", "Submit" and "Learn More" on their own.
  • Allow for translation: German and French labels run about 30% longer.

Limits are proposed and pending Marketing sign-off on the Character limits page.

Interaction timing

From the Figma dev handoff: Default → Hover 150ms ease-out, Hover → Default 100ms ease-in, Hover → Pressed instant, Pressed → Default 200ms ease-out. Changes to and from Disabled are instant.

Accessibility

  • Semantics: use <button> for actions and <a href> for navigation, even when they look the same (WCAG 4.1.2).
  • Focus visible: the Figma Focus state is a 2px ring 2px outside the button, in btn-focus-ring (gray-800 in light mode, white in dark mode) or white on media (WCAG 2.4.7). Use :focus-visible, so it shows for keyboard users.
  • Loading: set aria-busy="true" and keep the label in the markup, so the accessible name stays. Stop repeat clicks, and announce the outcome in a live region when the action finishes.
  • Keyboard: Enter and Space activate buttons; Enter activates links (WCAG 2.1.1).
  • Target size: tertiary, link and small icon buttons get an invisible hit area of at least 24px (WCAG 2.5.8). Small buttons are 32px high. Aim for 44px on touch.
  • Disabled: use aria-disabled="true" so the button stays in the tab order, and stop its click handler.
  • Label in name: an icon-only button's aria-label must describe the action. A visible label must be part of the accessible name (WCAG 2.5.3).
  • Contrast: the white Brand primary label is 5.5–6.8:1 on the deep blue at the left and centre of the image, but only 2.8:1 on the light blue at the right edge. Keep labels short so they sit on the darker part of the fill.

Acceptance checklist

  • Brand primary uses the image fill, not a gradient, and shows the hover blur. There's only one per view.
  • Every hierarchy has Default, Hover, Pressed, Focus and Disabled in light and dark, plus Loading for Primary, Secondary and IconButton.
  • Buttons over imagery use the On media surface.
  • The focus ring shows on keyboard focus for every button.
  • Loading buttons use aria-busy, keep their width and ignore repeat clicks.
  • Icon-only buttons have an accessible name.
  • Disabled buttons use aria-disabled and do nothing on click.
  • CTA groups stack at 767px and below.
  • Labels are verb-first, Title Case and within the limit.
