Overview

Banners carry a short, site-wide or page-level message. Figma has two:

  • Text banner ([section]-banner-text-wds-3.0): a slim strip with one line and a tertiary button, such as a pointer to terms and conditions.
  • Image banner ([section]-banner-image-wds-3.0): a promotional card with artwork, a title, one line of copy and a primary CTA, such as an event announcement.

Design guidance

Do

  • Use one banner at a time.
  • Use the text banner for information and the image banner for promotion.
  • Remove a promotional banner when the campaign ends.

Don't

  • Use a banner for urgent errors. It's easy to miss.
  • Put more than one action in a banner.
  • Stack a banner directly on top of a hero with the same message.

Light and dark theme

  • The text banner follows the theme through bg-subtle and text-primary.
  • The image banner card uses hard-coded light colours, so its text must stay dark. Put data-theme="light" on the card. Without it, dark mode turns the title white on a white card.

Microcopy and limits

SlotLimit
Text banner message45 characters, one line on desktop
Text banner action25 characters
Image banner title35 characters, 2 lines max on mobile
Image banner copy80 characters, one sentence
Image banner CTA30 characters

Limits are proposed, based on the Figma widths, and need Marketing sign-off.

  <a class="wds-btn wds-btn--tertiary" href="/terms">Terms &amp; Conditions<span class="wds-btn__circle">…</span></a>
</div>

<section class="wds-promo" aria-label="Tekion One 2027">
  <div class="wds-promo__card" data-theme="light">
    <div class="wds-promo__art" aria-hidden="true"></div>
    <div class="wds-promo__mark" aria-hidden="true"></div>
    <div class="wds-promo__body">
      <h2 class="wds-headline-3">Tekion One 2027 Is Coming</h2>
      <p class="wds-body-1-reg">Tekion One 2026 sold out. Be the first in line for 2027.</p>
    </div>
    <a class="wds-btn wds-btn--primary" href="/early-access">Get on the early access list</a>
  </div>
</section>

Replace .wds-promo__art and .wds-promo__mark with the exported Figma artwork for each campaign.

Accessibility

  • The text banner is plain content, not an alert. Don't give it role="alert".
  • Give the image banner a heading at the right level for the page, and an aria-label on its section.
  • Artwork is decorative: aria-hidden="true" or empty alt text.
  • If a banner can be dismissed, the close button needs an accessible name and the dismissal should be remembered.

Acceptance checklist

  • Text banner stacks on mobile.
  • Image banner card stays light in dark mode.
  • One action per banner, within the copy limits.
  • Decorative artwork is hidden from screen readers.
