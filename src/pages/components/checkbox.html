Overview

Checkboxes let people turn an option on or off, choose any number of options, or agree to a statement such as marketing consent. Figma has two components:

  • [c]-input-checkbox: the 20px box on its own (5 states).
  • [c]-input-checkbox-label: the box with a label, in single-line and multi-line formats (10 variants). Use this one in forms.

Variants and states

States: Default (1px border), Focus (2px border), Disabled, Checked (dark fill with a check) and Checked-disabled. The preview is live: select a box or tab to it.

Figma has no hover, error, indeterminate or checked-and-focused state. The CSS adds an outline ring for checked-and-focused (proposed). Error and indeterminate need Design before they can be used.

Design guidance

Do

  • Use checkboxes for independent choices, where any combination is valid.
  • Group related checkboxes in a fieldset with a question as its legend.
  • Leave consent boxes unchecked by default.

Don't

  • Use a checkbox when only one option can be chosen. Use radio buttons.
  • Use a checkbox to apply a setting immediately. That's a toggle.
  • Write labels as negatives ("Don't send me emails").

Responsive behaviour

Checkboxes don't change size between breakpoints. Labels wrap within their container. On mobile, stack options vertically with 12px between them, and keep the whole row tappable.

Light and dark theme

Checked boxes are dark with a white check in light mode, and white with a near-black check in dark mode. Like inputs, the unchecked background is 15% gray so the box works on gradients.

Microcopy and limits

  • Single-line labels: 50 characters max, sentence case, no end punctuation.
  • Consent labels: 250 characters max. Say exactly what people agree to and how to change their mind, and link to the privacy policy.
  • Group legends: a short question, such as "Which departments use Tekion today?"

Developer implementation

<label class="wds-check">
  <input type="checkbox" name="updates">
  <span>Send me product updates</span>
</label>

<fieldset class="wds-choice-group">
  <legend>Which departments use Tekion today?</legend>
  <label class="wds-check"><input type="checkbox" name="dept" value="sales"><span>Sales</span></label>
  …
</fieldset>

The native <input type="checkbox"> is restyled with appearance: none, so it keeps built-in keyboard support, form submission and screen reader announcements. Add .wds-check--multiline for consent copy.

Tokens

Accessibility

  • Wrap the input and text in one <label>, so the text is the accessible name and the whole row is clickable (WCAG 1.3.1, 2.5.8).
  • Tab moves focus and Space toggles (WCAG 2.1.1).
  • The check mark shows the state, not colour alone (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • The unchecked border (gray-300) is 1.82:1 on white, under the 3:1 WCAG 1.4.11 needs. It should move to gray-400.

Acceptance checklist

  • Native checkbox inside a <label>.
  • All five states match Figma in light and dark.
  • The box is aligned to the first line of multi-line labels.
  • Groups use a fieldset and legend.
  • Consent boxes start unchecked.
