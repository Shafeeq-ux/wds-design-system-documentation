Overview

A chip is a selectable pill for quick choices, such as a date or a filter, made in place without leaving the page. Figma has one component set, Chip, with five states and a Focus option that works on every state.

UseExampleSemantics
Pick one valueA session day: "Tue, Feb 17"Radio group
Pick any number of valuesAgenda filters: Sales, Service, PartsCheckbox group, or toggle buttons with aria-pressed

If tapping runs an action instead of choosing a value, use a Button, not a chip.

Variants and states

Select the chips in the live example to see the real interactions. The Hover, Pressed and Focus specimens are forced, so you can compare them side by side.

  • Default has no fill and a 1px border.
  • Hover adds a light fill and a darker border.
  • Pressed and Selected look the same: a solid fill with no border. Pressed is the moment of tapping; Selected is the chip staying chosen.
  • Disabled uses a faint border and muted label.
  • Focus shows a 2px ring 4px outside the chip, on any state.

Chips hug their label. In a row they sit 8px apart, so touch targets don't overlap.

Design guidance

Do

  • Use chips for a few short options people choose in place.
  • Show which chip is selected with the Selected state.
  • In a single-choice group, keep exactly one chip selected.

Don't

  • Use a chip to run an action. Use a button.
  • Use chips for long or many options. Use a dropdown or list.
  • Wrap a row of chips onto several lines on mobile.

Responsive behaviour

  • Chips are the same size at every breakpoint. 44px high meets the minimum touch target.
  • A row of chips (.wds-chips) scrolls sideways on narrow screens instead of wrapping.

Light and dark theme

  • Default has a gray-200 border and dark label in light mode, and a gray-700 border and white label in dark mode.
  • Pressed and Selected invert: gray-800 with a white label in light mode, white with a dark label in dark mode.

Microcopy and limits

  • One or two words, or a short date such as "Thu, Feb 18". 20 characters max (proposed).
  • Sentence case for words, and the same date format across a group.

Tokens

Accessibility

  • Semantics: use radios for single choice, checkboxes for multiple choice, or buttons with aria-pressed for toggles, so the selected state is announced (WCAG 4.1.2).
  • Group label: name the group with a <legend> or aria-label (WCAG 1.3.1).
  • Keyboard: Tab to the group; arrow keys move between radios, and Space toggles a checkbox or button (WCAG 2.1.1).
  • Focus visible: the 2px ring shows on keyboard focus (WCAG 2.4.7).
  • Not colour alone: Selected changes the fill and removes the border, and the state is also announced (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Boundary contrast: the Default border (gray-200) is about 1.4:1 on white. The label identifies the chip, but if chips sit without context, consider gray-400 (WCAG 1.4.11).

Acceptance checklist

  • All five states match Figma in light and dark, with the focus ring on keyboard focus.
  • Selected state is announced (radio, checkbox or aria-pressed).
  • The group has a label.
  • The chip doesn't change width between states.
  • Rows scroll sideways on mobile, with 8px between chips.
  • Labels are one or two words, or a short date.
