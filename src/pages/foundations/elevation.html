Overview

The WDS 3.0 Figma file has no shadow or effect styles yet. Until Design defines them, depth comes from surface colour:

LevelTokenLightDark
Page--wds-bg-page#ffffff#0a0c10
Subtle section--wds-bg-subtle#f5f7fa#141820
Recessed--wds-bg-recessed#eceef3#1e2230
Elevated (cards, menus)--wds-bg-elevated#ffffff#141820
Overlay (pop-ups)--wds-bg-overlay#ffffff#141820

In light mode, elevated and overlay surfaces are the same white as the page. Separate them with border-default until shadows exist.

Needed from Design

  • Shadow styles for at least three levels: raised cards, dropdowns and the header on scroll, and pop-ups.
  • Dark-mode guidance: whether shadows are dropped in favour of lighter surfaces.
  • A backdrop colour for the pop-up section's overlay.
