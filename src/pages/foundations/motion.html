Overview

The WDS 3.0 Figma file doesn't define motion yet. Several sections depend on it (Accordion, Carousel, Tabbed, Pop-up, and the header on scroll), so it's needed before those pages can be marked Stable.

Interim defaults

Until Design sets tokens, the documentation site and demos use these values. They're proposed, not approved.

UseDurationEasing
Hover and press colour changes150msease
Accordion expand, tab change200msease-out
Pop-up and drawer enter250mscubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)
Exit (any)150msease-in

Accessibility

  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Replace movement with an instant change or a short fade.
  • Anything that moves on its own for more than five seconds, such as an autoplaying carousel or background video, needs a visible pause control (WCAG 2.2.2).
  • Nothing flashes more than three times a second (WCAG 2.3.1).

Needed from Design

  • Duration and easing tokens.
  • Carousel autoplay timing and whether it's on by default.
  • Scroll-triggered animation rules, if any.
