Overview

The Logos page in Figma holds four groups: the Tekion brand and product-suite logos, product icons, compliance badges, and OEM and dealer logos used in trust sections. The brand and compliance files are shown here. OEM and dealer logos belong to their owners and are only available in Figma.

Brand logos

Dark backgrounds only. The ARC, AEC and APC wordmarks are white in Figma, so they disappear on light sections. A light-background version is needed from Design.

Usage guidance

Do

  • Use the teal Tekion wordmark on white, light gray or near-black backgrounds.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the "T".
  • Use the SVG so it stays sharp at every size.

Don't

  • Recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to a logo.
  • Put the logo on a busy photo or a gradient without checking contrast.
  • Rebuild a logo from type. The letterforms are custom.

Product icons

Seven product icons identify Core DMS, CRM, Virtual to Visit, DSE, Tekion Pay, Advanced Analytics and Payroll. They're full-colour illustrations with gradients, so they aren't exported here yet. Get them from the product-icons component in Figma.

Compliance badges

Badges for ISO/IEC 27001, ISO/IEC 27701, ISO 42001, SOC 1 and SOC 2. Each comes in a dark version (for light backgrounds) and a light version (for dark backgrounds).

  • Only show a badge for a certification Tekion currently holds, and link it to the trust or security page.
  • Don't change badge artwork. The certifying bodies control how their marks are used.
  • Give each badge alt text that names the standard, such as "ISO/IEC 27001 certified".

OEM and dealer logos

Partner logos are used in trust sections such as "Trusted by" rows. Use them only with permission and at a consistent height, and convert them to a single colour so no brand dominates. Files are in the OEM and Dealer sections of the Figma Logos page.
