Our standard

Every WDS page, component and section must meet WCAG 2.2 level AA in both light and dark themes, at every breakpoint. Accessibility is part of the definition of done, not a later pass.

Built into the system

  • Colour pairs are contrast-checked on every build. See Colour › Accessibility.
  • Components use native elements (button, input, details, dialog), so keyboard and screen reader support come for free.
  • Small buttons get invisible 24px+ hit areas.
  • Text styles use tokens that reflow at every breakpoint.
  • Every content slot has a character limit that leaves room for wrapping and zoom.

Known gaps

These are tracked on the relevant pages and need Design decisions:

  • Input and checkbox borders are below 3:1.
  • Teal text fails on light backgrounds.
  • Buttons have no Focus variant in Figma. A ring is proposed.
  • Motion and reduced-motion rules aren't defined.

Testing checklist

  • Run an automated check (axe DevTools or Lighthouse) on every new page.
  • Tab through the page: every control is reachable and the focus ring is always visible.
  • Check light and dark themes, and 200% zoom.
  • Check at 390px width: no horizontal scrolling, nothing cut off.
  • Use a screen reader (VoiceOver or NVDA) on new components and pop-ups.
