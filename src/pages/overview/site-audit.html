Summary

tekion.com currently mixes three generations of the design system:

  • WDS 3.0: sharp corners and thin outlines on cards and containers.
  • WDS 2.0: rounded corners, mostly 24px, on cards and containers.
  • WDS 1.0: the legacy site on tekion.ai. Some tekion.com menu links still redirect there.

This audit checked every page in the sitemap, plus the pages linked from the header menu, and sorted them by which system their content uses.

The shared shell is already 3.0. The header, mega menu and footer come from the same WDS components on every page, so the audit looks only at each page's content.

CMS collections

About 410 of the pages are items in Webflow CMS collections, such as blog posts and news articles. Every item in a collection uses the same template, so two items from each were measured and the result applied to the rest. Migrating one template moves every page in that collection at once.

  1. The WDS 1.0 pages. Digital Retail and Tekion AI are in the Products menu but send visitors to tekion.ai. Rebuild them on tekion.com with 3.0 sections, along with the referral form.

How pages were classified

  1. Inventory. All 455 URLs in tekion.com/sitemap.xml, plus 7 linked from the header menu but missing from the sitemap. /search is excluded because robots.txt blocks it.
  2. Grouping. Every page's HTML was fetched and its section structure compared, to separate one-off pages from CMS template items.
  3. Measurement. Each one-off page, and two items per CMS template, was opened at 1440 × 900. Every card-like box in the content area was measured: 180 × 100px or larger, with a background or a border, and outside the header, footer, menus and pop-ups.
  4. WDS 1.0 check. Pages that end up on tekion.ai are WDS 1.0. That site is a separate build: a different header, the Inter font, and none of the WDS components that every tekion.com page has. Its boxes are also square-cornered, so corner radius can't be used to tell 1.0 from 3.0.
  5. Decision. For tekion.com pages, a corner radius of 8px or more counts as rounded (2.0), and less than 8px as sharp (3.0). A page is 2.0 when at least 70% of its boxes are rounded, 3.0 when 30% or fewer are, and Mixed in between. It's also Mixed when it has one section that's all rounded and another that's all sharp.
  6. Design review. Design checked the results, and its corrections override the scan (see Design review corrections).

Limitations

  • A snapshot. Results reflect tekion.com on 17 October 2026. Re-run the audit after each migration.
  • Pages without cards, such as legal documents, can't be measured. They now take their template's version, confirmed by Design.
  • Pages with few boxes (fewer than 3) are classified with low confidence: FAQ, OEM Partners and the Refer sub-pages. Ask Design to confirm these too.
  • CMS items are sampled, not individually measured. An item with custom content blocks could differ from its template.
  • tekion.ai wasn't crawled. Only the three tekion.ai pages that tekion.com links to are included. The rest of tekion.ai is assumed to be WDS 1.0.
  • Corner radius is the only signal. Pages can match on radius and still differ in type, spacing or colour.
