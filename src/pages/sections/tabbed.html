Section overview

Tabbed sections switch between related views in one place, such as Ask / Decide / Act, or a before-and-after comparison.

Recommended placement

Mid-page, when 2 to 4 views share the same structure.

Audience

Evaluators who want to compare scenarios.

Goal

Show how the product behaves in different situations without repeating the layout.

Variants

UI shows product screens under each tab. 2-column compares "without" and "with" for each gap.

  • 2 column

Content structure and character limits

Limits are calculated from each slot's text style and its maximum width in Figma, so copy fits without breaking the layout. Type your copy into the last column to check it. Limits are proposed until Marketing signs them off.

Responsive layout behaviour

  • Tabs stay in one row on tablet. On mobile they scroll sideways if they don't fit.
  • The 2-column panel stacks on mobile, "without" first.

Section padding, gaps and widths come from the breakpoint tokens, so the spacing changes automatically at each breakpoint.

Light and dark theme

Both tabbed variants are dark in Figma.

Design guidance

Do

  • Pick the variant that fits the content, rather than forcing content into a variant.
  • Keep copy within the limits at mobile width, not just desktop.

Don't

  • Use tabs for content people need to see together.
  • Nest tabs inside tabs.

Accessibility

  • Use the tabs pattern: role="tablist", tab and tabpanel, with arrow keys moving between tabs.
  • The selected tab must be clear without colour alone.

Marketing handoff checklist

  • 2 to 4 tabs, labels within 20 characters.
  • Same content structure in every panel.
  • Section and variant chosen from Figma, with its node link.
  • Copy within every character limit on this page, checked at mobile width.
  • Eyebrow, H2 and description follow the heading rules.
  • Images supplied at 2× with alt text.
  • Reviewed in light and dark if the page supports both.
