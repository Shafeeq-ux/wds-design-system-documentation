Section overview

Feature sections explain how something works, either with a diagram and short descriptions (platform architecture) or with one large statement (content group regular).

Recommended placement

After the hero or between card grids, wherever a page needs to explain one idea in depth.

Audience

Evaluators who want to understand how the platform fits together.

Goal

Build understanding and lead to the relevant product page.

Variants

Use platform architecture to explain how products connect, and the statement (Headline 2 Regular) as a quiet bridge between two busier sections.

Content structure and character limits

Limits are calculated from each slot's text style and its maximum width in Figma, so copy fits without breaking the layout. Type your copy into the last column to check it. Limits are proposed until Marketing signs them off.

Token map

Every layer in this section that uses a Figma variable, taken from the Figma file. Platform architecture variant. Found in Figma: the 16px gaps in the cards and icon rows, the 6px padding on the icon circles, and the container's 32px top and bottom padding on Mobile aren't tokenised.

Variable modes

Like every WDS section, each variant applies the three variable collections: breakpoint for spacing and widths, typography for text sizes, and theme for colours. The same collections and modes carry over to Webflow through the Figma to Webflow plugin. See Figma to Webflow.

Responsive layout behaviour

  • The architecture diagram scales down on tablet and stacks the three descriptions below it on mobile.
  • The statement stays centred and wraps to more lines.

Section padding, gaps and widths come from the breakpoint tokens, so the spacing changes automatically at each breakpoint.

Light and dark theme

Platform architecture is dark in Figma; the statement is light.

Design guidance

Do

  • Pick the variant that fits the content, rather than forcing content into a variant.
  • Keep copy within the limits at mobile width, not just desktop.

Don't

  • Use the statement style for more than one paragraph.
  • Put small text inside the diagram image.

Accessibility

  • A diagram needs a text alternative that explains the relationships, not just its name.
  • The statement is a heading only if it introduces the content below it. Otherwise use a paragraph styled as Headline 2 Regular.

Marketing handoff checklist

  • Diagram exported as SVG, with a text alternative.
  • Statement within 4 lines on desktop.
  • Section and variant chosen from Figma, with its node link.
  • Each variant sets the breakpoint, typography and theme modes before it goes through the Figma to Webflow plugin.
  • Copy within every character limit on this page, checked at mobile width.
  • Eyebrow, H2 and description follow the heading rules.
  • Images supplied at 2× with alt text.
  • Reviewed in light and dark if the page supports both.
