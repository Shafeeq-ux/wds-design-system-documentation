Section overview

The footer closes every page with a final CTA, product links, three link columns, contact details, social links and legal information.

Recommended placement

Bottom of every page.

Audience

Visitors who reached the end, and anyone looking for company, legal or contact links.

Goal

Offer a last conversion point and help people find secondary pages.

Variants

One variant. The CTA band at the top can be swapped for a page-specific message.

Content structure and character limits

Limits are calculated from each slot's text style and its maximum width in Figma, so copy fits without breaking the layout. Type your copy into the last column to check it. Limits are proposed until Marketing signs them off.

Responsive layout behaviour

  • Desktop: products column plus four link columns.
  • Tablet: products on the left, link columns in a 2 × 2 grid.
  • Mobile: products full width, then link columns two to a row, then logo, social and legal stacked.

Section padding, gaps and widths come from the breakpoint tokens, so the spacing changes automatically at each breakpoint.

Light and dark theme

Always dark in Figma, like the header.

Design guidance

Do

  • Pick the variant that fits the content, rather than forcing content into a variant.
  • Keep copy within the limits at mobile width, not just desktop.

Don't

  • Add a second footer CTA.
  • Hide legal links behind a menu.

Accessibility

  • Use a footer landmark, and give each link group a heading or aria-label.
  • Social icons need accessible names ("Tekion on LinkedIn").
  • Privacy and cookie controls must be reachable by keyboard.

Marketing handoff checklist

  • Link list approved by Legal and Marketing.
  • CTA message for the page.
  • Current copyright year.
  • Section and variant chosen from Figma, with its node link.
  • Copy within every character limit on this page, checked at mobile width.
  • Eyebrow, H2 and description follow the heading rules.
  • Images supplied at 2× with alt text.
  • Reviewed in light and dark if the page supports both.
