Section overview

Video sections present one video, either with a title and description above it or as a bare player.

Recommended placement

Anywhere a demo or story is the best explanation, usually after the hero.

Audience

Visitors who prefer watching to reading.

Goal

Show the product in action.

Variants

AI video single has a title and description above the player. Video is the player alone, for use inside other layouts.

  • ai single
  • player

Content structure and character limits

Limits are calculated from each slot's text style and its maximum width in Figma, so copy fits without breaking the layout. Type your copy into the last column to check it. Limits are proposed until Marketing signs them off.

Responsive layout behaviour

  • The player keeps 16:9 and fills the container at every size.
  • Player controls use the video-player padding tokens, which shrink on mobile.

Section padding, gaps and widths come from the breakpoint tokens, so the spacing changes automatically at each breakpoint.

Light and dark theme

Both variants are light in Figma. Player controls sit on the video, so they use static colours.

Design guidance

Do

  • Pick the variant that fits the content, rather than forcing content into a variant.
  • Keep copy within the limits at mobile width, not just desktop.

Don't

  • Autoplay with sound.
  • Put the only copy of important information in the video.

Accessibility

  • Provide captions for all speech and a transcript link (WCAG 1.2.2).
  • Use a poster image and never autoplay with sound.
  • Every control (play, mute, full screen) needs an accessible name.

Marketing handoff checklist

  • Video file or embed, poster image, captions file.
  • Title and description within limits.
  • Section and variant chosen from Figma, with its node link.
  • Copy within every character limit on this page, checked at mobile width.
  • Eyebrow, H2 and description follow the heading rules.
  • Images supplied at 2× with alt text.
  • Reviewed in light and dark if the page supports both.
