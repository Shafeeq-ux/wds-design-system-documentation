Section overview

Card grids present a set of parallel items, such as products, benefits or articles, with the same structure so people can scan and compare. The Figma page has 11 grid variants from 2 to 8 cards.

Recommended placement

Mid-page, after the hero or a feature section. Use one or two card grids per page at most.

Audience

Visitors comparing options or looking for the part that applies to them.

Goal

Help people find the product, benefit or article that matters to them and move on to it.

Variants

Use 3-column for three key benefits, 4-column for product families, grid 6 and grid 8 for longer benefit lists, explore more for related content, and the video grids for session libraries.

Content structure and character limits

Limits are calculated from each slot's text style and its maximum width in Figma, so copy fits without breaking the layout. Type your copy into the last column to check it. Limits are proposed until Marketing signs them off.

Variable modes

Like every WDS section, each variant applies the three variable collections: breakpoint for spacing and widths, typography for text sizes, and theme for colours. The same collections and modes carry over to Webflow through the Figma to Webflow plugin. See Figma to Webflow.

Responsive layout behaviour

  • Desktop shows 2 to 5 columns using the card-width tokens (see Grid & breakpoints).
  • On tablet, 3-column cards become rows with text on the left and image on the right. 4-column grids become two columns.
  • On mobile, every grid becomes one column, except 5-column grids, which can fit two.

Section padding, gaps and widths come from the breakpoint tokens, so the spacing changes automatically at each breakpoint.

Light and dark theme

Most card grids are dark in Figma. Card surfaces use bg-page with border-subtle dividers, so they work in light too.

Design guidance

Do

  • Pick the variant that fits the content, rather than forcing content into a variant.
  • Keep copy within the limits at mobile width, not just desktop.

Don't

  • Mix cards with and without images in one grid.
  • Make one card much longer than the rest.
  • Use a card grid for a single item.

Accessibility

  • Each card title is a heading (usually h3) under the section's h2.
  • If the whole card is clickable, use one link and stretch its hit area. Don't nest several links.
  • Repeated link labels like "Learn more" need hidden context: "Learn more about Core DMS".

Marketing handoff checklist

  • All cards have the same parts, filled in.
  • Card titles within 60 characters (3 columns) or 45 (4 columns).
  • Images share one aspect ratio.
  • Section and variant chosen from Figma, with its node link.
  • Each variant sets the breakpoint, typography and theme modes before it goes through the Figma to Webflow plugin.
  • Copy within every character limit on this page, checked at mobile width.
  • Eyebrow, H2 and description follow the heading rules.
  • Images supplied at 2× with alt text.
  • Reviewed in light and dark if the page supports both.
