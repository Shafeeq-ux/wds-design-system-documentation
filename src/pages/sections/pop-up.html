Section overview

Pop-ups show extra content in a dialog over the page, such as a video, a testimonial or a product overview, without leaving it.

Recommended placement

Opened from a button or card. Never on page load.

Audience

Visitors who chose to see more about one item.

Goal

Give depth on demand while keeping people on the page.

Variants

Video carousel, testimonial and video play media. Card and content image show long-form product overviews.

Content structure and character limits

Limits are calculated from each slot's text style and its maximum width in Figma, so copy fits without breaking the layout. Type your copy into the last column to check it. Limits are proposed until Marketing signs them off.

Responsive layout behaviour

  • On desktop the dialog is centred over a dimmed page.
  • On mobile it fills the screen, with the close button at the top.
  • Long content scrolls inside the dialog, not the page behind it.

Section padding, gaps and widths come from the breakpoint tokens, so the spacing changes automatically at each breakpoint.

Light and dark theme

All pop-ups are dark in Figma.

Design guidance

Do

  • Pick the variant that fits the content, rather than forcing content into a variant.
  • Keep copy within the limits at mobile width, not just desktop.

Don't

  • Open a pop-up automatically.
  • Put a form people must complete inside a pop-up on mobile.

Accessibility

  • Use dialog with showModal() so focus is trapped and Escape closes it.
  • Move focus into the dialog when it opens and back to the trigger when it closes.
  • The close button needs an accessible name, and the dialog needs a label (its title).

Marketing handoff checklist

  • What opens the pop-up.
  • Video files and captions.
  • Backdrop colour (not yet defined: see Elevation).
  • Section and variant chosen from Figma, with its node link.
  • Copy within every character limit on this page, checked at mobile width.
  • Eyebrow, H2 and description follow the heading rules.
  • Images supplied at 2× with alt text.
  • Reviewed in light and dark if the page supports both.
