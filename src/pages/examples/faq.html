Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is F&I Manager AI?

    F&I Manager AI is an intelligent agent within Tekion’s AI-native platform that accelerates deal progression and mitigates compliance friction.

  • What is T1 and how does it work?

    T1 is the AI layer built into Automotive Retail Cloud. It understands your whole dealership and can answer questions and take action.

  • What happens to my existing data?

    Your data is migrated to ARC during implementation, with validation at every milestone.

  • Can T1 actually take action or does it just show me data?

    T1 can draft, schedule, send and trigger next steps, with approvals you control.

  • Is my customer and payment data secure?

    Yes. ARC is SOC 1/2 Type II, ISO 27001 and ISO/IEC 42001 certified.
